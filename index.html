I’ve been where you are.

I’ve built and operated consumer businesses from the ground up.

I founded a business that grew to more than 100 locations. I know what it takes to create something people love and how much growth happens because one person brings another in. That growth is real. It just hasn’t had infrastructure.
Could a business do more than create a transaction? Could it make it easier for people to do something good for someone else?
Giving feels good. Being thought of feels good. And one act of generosity can inspire another.
I wanted to know if that feeling could become a behavior.
So I built a business around it.
Lifehood · Los Angeles · 2020

Before Love It Forward, there was Lifehood.

In 2020, I opened a wellness center in Los Angeles around one simple idea: make it easy to do something good for someone else. Customers could anonymously send an experience to someone they cared about. The receiver discovered who had thought of them. Then they could choose someone else.
And something happened.
People didn’t continue because we paid them to. They continued because someone else came to mind.
That behavior became the foundation for Love It Forward.
